# SpudgerHQ

**Phone repair shop software for faster intake, clearer repair tickets, and simpler daily workflow.**

SpudgerHQ helps small phone repair shops manage the work that happens every day: checking in customers, creating repair tickets, tracking job status, keeping customer records organized, and closing out repairs without relying on paper tickets or disconnected tools.

Visit: https://spudgerhq.com

## Built for Small Phone Repair Shops

SpudgerHQ is designed for solo repair shop owners and small repair teams that need a simpler way to run the front desk and repair workflow.

Instead of juggling spreadsheets, paper tickets, generic POS tools, and scattered customer notes, SpudgerHQ gives your shop one repair-focused system for the full job flow.

## What SpudgerHQ Helps With

- Fast repair intake
- Repair ticket creation and tracking
- Customer and device records
- Issue notes and condition notes
- Technician handoffs
- Repair status visibility
- Checkout workflow support
- Inventory and parts workflow support

## The Workflow

A customer walks in with a phone issue.

Your team checks them in, captures the device details, creates a repair ticket, updates the job as work progresses, and closes out the repair when it is ready for pickup.

SpudgerHQ keeps that workflow clearer from intake to completion.
